Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Shorts Bot ("the app") is a personal automation tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel @WeshAlTrend ("we"). It runs on the owner's own computer, creates short videos and uploads them to that channel. This policy explains what the app does with Google and YouTube data.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

  • When the channel owner connects a Google account, Google gives the app an OAuth access token and refresh token for one permission only: uploading videos to YouTube (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload).
  • The app does not request or read names, email addresses, contacts, watch history, comments, subscribers, analytics or any other account data, and it never accesses anyone else's account.

How the information is used

The token is used only to upload videos the app has created to the connected channel, together with each video's title, description, tags, category, privacy setting and scheduled publish time.

Storage and security

The tokens are stored encrypted in the credential store of the n8n automation software on the owner's computer. The videos and their text are stored on the same computer. The app sends account data only to Google's YouTube API. It downloads free stock footage from public stock libraries but sends them no user data.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose user data to anyone. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking or cookies, and this website sets no cookies.

Retention and deletion

The tokens are kept only while the channel is connected. Deleting the credential in n8n, or revoking access as described below, removes the app's access immediately; the stored tokens are then deleted. Video files stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time from the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After that the app can no longer upload anything.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
